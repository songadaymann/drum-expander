Drum loop → 6 MIDI tracks

drum2midi run on loops from your Splice library. Press play, then turn drums on and off to check each one against the original. The mix slider fades between the loop and the MIDI (rendered through a stock General MIDI kit, so judge timing and hits, not tone). Taller, darker marks are louder hits: velocity now follows each hit's actual loudness. In HATS, outlined marks are open hats. In TOMS, high, mid and low toms sit at the top, middle and bottom of the lane.

Source loops are Splice samples, so keep this page local. MIDI files for each loop are in results/<loop>/midi/.
